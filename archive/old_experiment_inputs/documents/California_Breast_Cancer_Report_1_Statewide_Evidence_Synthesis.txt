California Breast Cancer Report 1: Statewide Evidence Synthesis

Summary
Breast cancer remains the most commonly diagnosed cancer among women in
California. Age-adjusted incidence has been broadly stable over the last
decade, while mortality has declined modestly, driven largely by earlier
detection and improvements in systemic therapy.

Screening
Mammography uptake varies by region and by insurance status. Counties with
integrated community outreach report higher rates of on-schedule screening
than counties relying on clinic-initiated reminders alone.

Recommendation
Sustain investment in population-level screening reminders and continue to
monitor incidence and mortality trends by county and by demographic group.
