California Breast Cancer Report 2: Promotora Community Navigation

Summary
Promotora (community health worker) navigation programs connect patients to
screening, diagnostic follow-up, and treatment. This report reviews outcomes
from navigation programs operating in several California counties.

Findings
Patients enrolled in promotora navigation completed diagnostic follow-up after
an abnormal screen at higher rates than patients receiving usual care. The
effect was largest among patients facing language or transportation barriers.

Recommendation
Expand promotora navigation in underserved counties and standardize referral
tracking so that follow-up completion can be measured consistently statewide.
