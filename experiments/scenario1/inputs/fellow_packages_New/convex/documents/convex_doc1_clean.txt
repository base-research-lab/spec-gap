Convergence Rate of Projected Subgradient Method
with Time-varying Step-sizes
Zhihan Zhu · Yanhao Zhang · Yong Xia
Received: date / Accepted: date
Abstract We establish the optimal ergodic convergence rate for the classical
projected subgradient method with time-varying step-sizes. This convergence
rate remains the same even if we slightly increase the weight of the most recent
points, thereby relaxing the ergodic sense.
Keywords Subgradient method · step-size · ergodic convergence rate ·
nonsmooth convex optimization
