Analysis of Favorable Injection-production Area Near the Fault
Where the Directional Wells Were Deployed
Lili Meng
Fifth Oil Production Plant of Daqing Oilfield Co., Ltd., Daqing, China
Abstract. Through the large-scale application of directional well technology, X development zone has
achieved good potential tapping effect near the fault. However, with the increase of production batches of
directional wells, the proportion of high-yield wells decreases, and the difficulty of well location optimization
increases gradually. In order to further improve the recovery near the fault, under the condition of large-scale
development of sand body in the study area, this paper focuses on the analysis of the deployment area
conducive to the high yield of directional wells from the perspective of injection-production relationship.
Combined with the development practice of 48 directional wells that have been deployed and have achieved
good development results, the well distribution effect is evaluated from three aspects of well pattern, well
spacing and injection-production location, and the deployment principle of directional wells are determined.
The favorable injection-production area for the deployment of directional wells near the fault is summarized,
which indicates the direction for further improving oil recovery near the fault in the old oilfield.
Keywords: Fault; directional well; injection-production relationship; well location deployment.
1. Introduction
X Development Zone is a structural reservoir and
structural-lithologic reservoir. At present, it has entered
a period of ultra-high water cut development. The
remaining oil is highly scattered, only enriched locally,
and it is difficult to tap potential. In the past, when the
dense well pattern was deployed near the fault, in order
to avoid the loss of the target layer, the designed well
position was far away from the fault as far as possible,
resulting in low well pattern density near the fault and
imperfect injection and production relationship, which
was the main remaining oil enrichment area. Since 2013,
based on the results of well-seismic combined with fine
fault interpretation, 48 large displacement directional
wells have been deployed near the fault of X
development zone, and good development results have
been achieved, which broadens the technical path of
oilfield potential tapping in the late stage of ultra-high
water cut. In practice, it is found that the structure,
reservoir and injection-production relationship jointly
affect the development effect of large displacement
directional wells near the fault. This paper analyzes the
three aspects of well pattern, well spacing and injection-
production location, and summarizes the laws that they
have a positive impact on the development effect of
directional wells, providing guidance for the
popularization and application of large displacement
directional wells.
2. Analysis and evaluation of directional
well production effect
The evaluation standard of directional well production
effect is established. Because of the good correlation
between the initial cumulative production, the initial daily
oil production, the initial water content and the predicted
ten-year cumulative production of the directional well
with large displacement, the evaluation standard of
directional well production effect is established by
determining the parameters.
The wells that have been put into operation in X
development zone are divided into four categories : the
first type well has the best production effect, the second
type well is relatively good, the third type well meets the
design requirements, and the fourth type well has
relatively poor production effect and low production,
which does not meet the design requirements ( Table 1 ).

⑴Effect of well spacing on well distribution : basic well
The directional well and the basic well with relatively
good effect are more than 200 meters, and the directional
well is more than 250 meters away from the basic well.
⑵ Effect of well spacing on well distribution : basic well
The distance between primary wells has little effect on
the overall effect of directional well layout. When the
primary well is more than 60 meters, and the primary
well is more than 100 meters, it can meet the design
requirements of directional wells.
⑶Effect of well spacing on well distribution:secondary
well
The distance between the secondary wells has little effect
on the overall effect of directional well layout. The
secondary wells are more than 60 meters, and the
secondary wells are more than 100 meters, which can
meet the design requirements of directional wells.
⑷ Effect of well spacing on well distribution : third infill
well
Third infill well within 100 meters from the directional
well will affect the well distribution effect.
4. The position of directional wells with
different distribution effect in
injection-production well pattern
One kind of directional well is more than 200 meters away
from the foundation well and is located outside the main
flow line of injection and production of the basic well.
If the directional well is more than 170 meters away from
the first, second and third wells, it will not affect the well
distribution effect at any injection-production location.
The second type of directional wells are more than 100
meters away from the foundation wells, and are located
outside the main streamline of injection and production of
the foundation wells; directional well is more than 130
meters from first infill well and more than 100 meters
from second well; when the distance from polymer
flooding wells is more than 240 meters, the well
distribution effect will be relatively good. The three types
of directional wells are close to oil wells or wells, and
most of them are located on the main flow line of water
injection. If the four types of directional wells are within
200 meters from the basic well or are located on the
injection-production relationship main streamline of the
basic well , the well distribution effect will become worse .

5. Conclusions and recommendations
1. Basic well pattern has the greatest impact on directional
well deployment, while first, second and third wells have
relatively small impact on directional well deployment.
2. It is recommended that directional well locations be
deployed over 180 metres from the basic well and over
200 metres from the basic well and outside the main
stream line.
