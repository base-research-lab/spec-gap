Abstract
The advent of large language models (LLMs) such as ChatGPT has potential implications for psychological therapies such as
cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT). We systematically investigated whether LLMs could recognize an unhelpful thought, examine
its validity, and reframe it to a more helpful one. LLMs currently have the potential to offer reasonable suggestions for the
identification and reframing of unhelpful thoughts but should not be relied on to lead CBT delivery.
