California Breast Cancer Report 3: Diagnostic Equity

Summary
This report examines equity in the diagnostic interval, defined as the time
from an abnormal screening result to a definitive diagnosis. Shorter, more
uniform diagnostic intervals are associated with better outcomes.

Findings
The diagnostic interval varied substantially across facilities. Patients at
facilities with dedicated navigation and reserved diagnostic slots reached
diagnosis faster than patients at facilities without them. Disparities by
insurance status narrowed where reserved diagnostic capacity was available.

Recommendation
Fund reserved diagnostic capacity and navigation at facilities serving
high-need populations, and report diagnostic-interval metrics publicly so
that facilities can be compared on a consistent basis.
